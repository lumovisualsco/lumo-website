The Lumo Experience

At Lumo, we believe your photos should feel like you. Our approach is relaxed, intentional, and personal. We take our time, guide you through every pose, and create a comfortable space where genuine moments can happen naturally.

Whether we're capturing a milestone, your growing family, or simply this season of your life, our goal is to create timeless images that feel meaningful long after your session is over.

Still thinking?

Not quite ready to book? That’s okay.

Most of our clients tell us they’ve never had professional photos taken before. Feeling a little unsure is completely normal — and exactly why we built the experience the way we did.

I’m awkward in front of the camera

You’re not alone. We’ll guide you through every pose and prompt so it feels relaxed, natural, and fun. No pressure to know what to do.

What if the weather is bad?

Washington weather happens. If conditions make the session unsafe or unsuitable, we reschedule at no extra cost.

I don’t want to commit to a big session

Start with a Mini Session. It’s a gentle, low-pressure way to experience Lumo and see how natural it feels.

I just want to talk first

No problem. Use the inquiry form below and tell us you’re exploring. We’ll answer questions and help you decide if it feels right — no obligation.

Questions we hear often

Where are you located?

We’re based in Lynnwood, Washington and proudly serve Lynnwood, Seattle, Bellevue, Everett, Snohomish County, and surrounding areas. Travel outside these areas may be available for an additional fee.

What should I wear?

Neutral and earthy colors, coordinated outfits (instead of matching), and avoid large logos or busy patterns. Send us your outfit ideas before the session if you’d like feedback.

Can I bring my pet?

Absolutely! We love including pets whenever the location allows. Just let us know ahead of time.

When will I receive my gallery?

Your online gallery is delivered within 3–5 weeks. Rush editing may be available if you need photos sooner.

Do you provide raw photos?

No. Every image is professionally edited to reflect our style and ensure the highest quality.
